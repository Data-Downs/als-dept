# Demo Script — Agentic Legibility Stack

## Voiceover Narrative

> Now, let's return to Anna and show you what her future agentic experience of government might look like
>
> I want to show you a prototype that joins up government around Anna, and uses agents to make government work proactively for her
>
> She can choose an interaction style with agents to suit her needs. From a cautious DOT to a more autonomous MAX
>
> She can monitor tasks and delegate them to agents
>
> She can interact with GOV.UK by typing, speaking, or tapping
>
> She can form plans that brings together all of government around what's happening in her life
>
> She can easily work out what support she can get, based on her data
>
> Instead of filling out forms and awaiting an outcome, agents can apply for things on her behalf, and she can choose how much oversight to have
>
> All in one place, for you
