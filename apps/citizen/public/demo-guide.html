GOV.UK · agentic citizen experience

Demo guide

Five curated citizens, each chosen for how much of the cohort they exercise. Pick one to show a different corner of everyday life with government.

How the demo runs

  1. Open the citizen switcher (top right) and choose a person.
  2. Read their briefing — who they are, which agents they already have, and the best way to show them off.
  3. Press Start the demo. Their situation loads and Dot opens proactively — leading with what's overdue and what they're owed.
  4. Follow the numbered moves below (or the same list on the briefing card).

The affluent couple and their fleet

Mary & Hugo Summers · 64 · London

A comfortable retired couple at the estate-planning stage of life. Between them they run a three-car household — a 2023 BMW, a 2022 Audi Q5 and a cherished 1965 Jaguar E-Type — exactly the household with quiet admin slipping through the cracks.

Three-car household A 1965 classic car Approaching retirement Estate & power of attorney
Agents:DotMiles

How to show it off

  1. Open Miles and ask him to check all three cars — he flags the Audi's July MOT and recognises the 1965 Jaguar as a historic vehicle (tax and MOT exempt).
  2. Type any made-up registration to show the generator conjuring a complete, coherent vehicle for any plate.
  3. Ask Miles whether you could drive a large motorhome on your licence, to show the entitlement check.

The family leaning on support

Fatima Nowak · 43 · Luton

Fatima works part-time in school catering; Tomasz is a warehouse supervisor. Three children — Kasia (15), Adam (11, with an EHCP), Lily (5). The family for whom a missed entitlement or deadline genuinely bites: the vulnerable-first story.

Three children Child Benefit 16th-birthday cliff Adam's EHCP Free school meals & childcare
Agents:DotFayCass · Citizens Advice

How to show it off

  1. Read Dot's opener — she leads with the Child Benefit cliff: it stops at Kasia's 16th birthday unless Fatima confirms she's staying in education. The vigilance beat.
  2. Open Fay and ask her to keep Kasia's Child Benefit running; ask about Adam's EHCP and free school meals.
  3. The third-party beat. In the tray, open Cass — a benefits agent from Citizens Advice, not government. Commissioning her shows the consent moment: exactly what access you're granting, certified by GDS and revocable any time. This is the £24bn advocacy-at-scale idea made real.

Bereavement — the hardest week, carried

Sarah Okafor · 58 · Chelmsford

Sarah's husband David died in February. A will, a £645,000 estate, probate not yet started, pensions to unwind — the admin that lands at the worst moment. Grace is already with her, carrying it so Sarah doesn't have to hold it while she grieves.

Recent bereavement Tell Us Once Probate & inheritance tax Pensions & Bereavement Support
Agents:DotGraceIris · Cruse

How to show it off

  1. Notice the tone shift — Grace is gentle and unhurried. The emotional centrepiece.
  2. Open Grace and let her carry it: Tell Us Once notifies every department at once, then Bereavement Support Payment. After she files something, tap the receipt's "Undo" for the safe-to-be-wrong layer.
  3. A third party standing beside government. In the tray, open Iris — a bereavement companion from Cruse, a charity. The same cohort, government-built and not, coordinating on one event: Grace for the admin, Iris for the human part.

Two things best shown as a new user. Choose New user (start fresh) to demonstrate:
