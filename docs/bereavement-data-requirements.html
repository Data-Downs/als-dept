Services triggered

Six services across four departments, executed in sequence. Each service cascades data to the next — Sarah provides information once.

Step 1
General Register Office

Register death

Legal registration within 5 days. Unlocks everything else.

Step 2
General Register Office

Death certificate

Official certificate posted to Sarah's address.

Step 3
DWP

Tell Us Once

Notify HMRC, DWP, DVLA, Passport Office, council, electoral register in one go.

Step 4
DWP

Bereavement Support Payment

£3,500 lump sum plus 18 monthly payments of £350.

Step 5
HM Courts & Tribunals Service

Probate

Grant of probate as named executor of David's will.

Step 6
HMRC

Inheritance tax return

IHT400 filed. Estate £645k is below threshold — no tax due.

Field deduplication

Each service defines its own field names for the same data. The field merger maps all variants to canonical fields — the agent asks once, fans out to all services.

What this means for Sarah

Without deduplication, Sarah would answer 87 separate questions across 6 government services — many asking for the same information in slightly different ways. With the field merger, she confirms 20 pieces of information, and of those, 13 are already in her wallet. She only needs to provide 7 new pieces of data.

The bottom line

A bereaved citizen — someone in grief, with no experience of government admin — needs to provide 7 pieces of information to complete 6 services across 4 departments. The remaining 13 fields are either already in her digital wallet or retrieved from government systems with her consent. This is the difference between an agentic service and a set of forms.

Consent model

Sarah grants consent once per data-sharing scope. The agent explains what will be shared, with whom, and why — then waits for approval.

Consent scope Data shared Recipients Duration
Tell Us Once notification Name, address, NI number (Sarah's and David's), relationship, date of death HMRC, DWP, DVLA, Passport Office, Chelmsford Council, Electoral Register One-time
Death registration Deceased details, cause of death, medical certificate, informant identity General Register Office One-time
BSP claim Identity, death certificate, NI contributions, marriage proof, bank details DWP One-time
Probate application Executor identity, will, estate valuation, death certificate, IHT reference HMCTS One-time
IHT return Executor identity, deceased details, estate valuation, gifts, will details HMRC One-time
