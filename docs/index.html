Agentic Legibility Stack

A working prototype demonstrating how UK government services could be delivered through AI agents — with full transparency, auditability, and citizen control.

This project addresses a fundamental question: how should government services work in a world where citizens interact through AI agents rather than web forms?

The challenge is twofold. Citizens need a better way to navigate the complexity of government — one that works across departments, anticipates their needs, and handles the bureaucratic burden on their behalf. And departments need a clear framework for making their services accessible to AI agents safely and transparently.

This stack provides both sides. A citizen-facing prototype shows how the future of GOV.UK could feel. An administrative studio shows departments exactly what they need to publish — and what happens when they do.

Platform One

Legibility Studio

An administrative platform that helps government departments make their services legible to AI agents. For agents to interact with services safely, those services must publish structured, machine-readable artefacts.

Inventory

Service Inventory

A complete catalogue of government services, classified by typology — benefits, entitlements, registrations, obligations, applications — with their current readiness for agentic interaction.

Completeness

Artefact Completeness

Each service requires four artefacts to be legible: a manifest, a policy ruleset, a state model, and consent rules. The studio tracks which exist, which are missing, and what needs to be authored.

Analysis

Gap Analysis

A department-level view showing how ready each service is for agentic delivery. Identifies precisely what work remains — which artefacts are missing, which rules are incomplete, which journeys lack consent models.

Audit

Evidence & Audit

Every interaction between an agent and a government service generates an immutable evidence trail. The studio provides full replay capability — what happened, when, what data was shared, and what decisions were made.

What departments need to publish

  • Service Manifest
  • Policy Ruleset
  • State Model
  • Consent Rules
  • State Instructions
Platform Two

Citizen Experience

A prototype of how the future GOV.UK app could work through agentic technology — where citizens interact conversationally with an AI agent that navigates government services on their behalf.

Interaction

Conversational Interaction

Citizens describe their situation in plain language. The agent understands what services are relevant, checks eligibility, collects the necessary information, and submits applications — explaining every step as it goes.

Orchestration

Cross-Department Orchestration

Life events rarely respect departmental boundaries. A bereavement, a new child, or a job loss touches multiple departments simultaneously. The agent coordinates across all of them from a single conversation.

Proactive

Proactive Notifications

Rather than requiring citizens to know which services exist, the agent identifies relevant entitlements and obligations based on their circumstances — surfacing what matters before the citizen has to search for it.

Transparency

Full Transparency

Every action the agent takes is visible to the citizen — what data was shared, with whom, why, and what the outcome was. Citizens maintain control throughout, with the ability to revoke consent at any point.

The vision is a single, personalised interface to all of government — where an agent handles the complexity of navigating between departments, and the citizen simply describes what they need.
