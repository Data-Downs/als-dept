# Two-Week Handover Plan

*Your checklist for filling in this pack before you leave. Front-loaded on purpose — capture while your memory and access are fresh, then shape and test. Short daily sessions beat one heroic dump.*

**The goal:** by your last day, a smart colleague who isn't you can open the `handover/` folder, understand the project, run the demo, and know what to do next — without a meeting.

**The done test:** hand the folder to someone, say nothing, and see if they can explain the project back to you and start the demo. If yes, you're finished.

---

## Week 1 — Capture (get it out of your head)

Don't polish. Rough notes are fine. The enemy is the blank page, not bad prose. If talking is easier than typing, record a voice memo and clean it up later — or ask Claude to interview you and write it up.

- [ ] **Day 1 — Decisions brain-dump.** Open `01-DECISION-LOG.md`. Fill in the *why* for D1–D6, add any I missed, and jot the product/strategic decisions. Don't stop to make it neat.
- [ ] **Day 2 — Open questions & "looks wrong but isn't".** Finish the bottom of `01-DECISION-LOG.md`. These fade fastest from memory.
- [ ] **Day 3 — State of play.** Open `02-STATE-OF-PLAY.md`. Correct the pre-filled facts, then be honest about what's solid vs. fragile and where the gaps are.
- [ ] **Day 4 — Roadmap.** Finish `02-STATE-OF-PLAY.md` — what you'd build next and why. Rough bullets are gold.
- [ ] **Day 5 — People & promises.** Fill `03-STAKEHOLDER-MAP.md`: who cares, what's been promised, the politics, who to contact for what.

## Week 2 — Shape & validate

- [ ] **Day 6 — Tidy the capture.** Read back Week 1, turn rough notes into clear sentences. Claude can help polish.
- [ ] **Day 7 — Runbook.** Fill `04-HOW-TO-RUN-IT.md` by actually running the demo from scratch and writing down everything you "just knew".
- [ ] **Day 8 — Orientation.** Finish `00-READ-ME-FIRST.md` last, now that you know what it's pointing at. Get the "if you only read one more thing" pointer right.
- [ ] **Day 9 — The test.** Give the folder to a colleague. Watch them try to understand it and run the demo. Write down every question they ask — those are your gaps.
- [ ] **Day 10 — Fix the gaps, then stop.** Fill what the test exposed. Have the handover conversations listed in `03-STAKEHOLDER-MAP.md`. Done.

## Optional but high-value

- [ ] **Record a 10–15 min screen-recording** walking through the best demo path, narrating what you're doing and why. For many successors this is worth more than any document — they can watch you *do* it.
- [ ] **Leave the key (safely).** Make sure whoever continues this knows how to get/replace the `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` and any hosting credentials — via your org's proper secret-sharing, never in these files.

---

*Tip: you don't have to do this alone. Claude can interview you for any of these documents — just say "interview me for the decision log" and answer the questions out loud.*
